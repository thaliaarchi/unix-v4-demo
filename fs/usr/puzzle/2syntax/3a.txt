Hint: 0x hexadecimal literals were introduced in UNIX V7. Before then, only
decimal and octal literals were supported.

Octal was the preferred base for expressing bit patterns, a tradition inherited
from earlier systems. Despite the 16-bit word size not being divisible by 3 (3
bits per octal digit), all PDP-11 documentation used octal. Note that the
switches on the PiDP-11 front panel are colored in groups of three, denoting
octal digits.
